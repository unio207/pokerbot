## 3. Who is eligible?

### Takeaway
Students currently enrolled at U.S. or Canadian universities, plus class of 2025 or 2026 graduates of those universities, competing solo or in teams of two; nominally 18+, with a parental-consent route for under-18s. Not open to the general public.

### Cited Findings
- Rules §1.1: "Participation is open to currently enrolled students at any university in the United States and Canada" and "students of U.S. and Canadian universities from the class of 2025 or 2026." — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- School affiliation is verified through a school email address — [Official site](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/)
- Age 18 or older; participants under 18 need parental/guardian consent to compete in a regional, via a consent form sent upon acceptance (§1.2) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Teams are one or two members; teammates need not attend the same school; each participant may compete in only one regional (§1.4) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Excluded: gamemasters, judges, and employees/officers/directors/contractors of the Organizer, Hosts and affiliates, plus their immediate family and household members (§1.3) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Participants must be legally eligible under applicable law; use of OpenAI services must occur from a supported country; void where prohibited (§1.6) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Original launch eligibility was U.S. universities only; early coverage reflects that — [Digg](https://digg.com/ai/smdduv3c); later expanded to Canada — [Sequoia on X](https://x.com/sequoia/status/2115826610914103671)
- No poker experience is required, per Sequoia: "You don't need to be a poker pro, or even have any poker experience, to win No Limit... The starter kit handles the rules and the ICM math." — [Sequoia on X](https://x.com/sequoia/status/2117313159342268468)

### Inferences
- "Apply to the closest regional event" implies no regional residency rule, but all three regionals are in the U.S. and require in-person attendance, so Canadian students must travel to a U.S. regional.
- No degree-level restriction (undergrad vs. grad) or major restriction is stated in the sources read.

### Gaps
- Whether citizenship/visa status matters for prize payment (beyond "legally eligible" and tax paperwork) is not detailed.
- Whether travel to regionals (as opposed to Finals) is covered is not stated; the rules say participants bear their own expenses.

## 4. Prizes and how winners are determined

### Takeaway
Finals prizes are $100,000 / $50,000 / $25,000 per team for 1st/2nd/3rd, and each top-5 team at each regional gets $5,000; winners are decided purely by automated bot performance (weighted points across six tournaments). No job offers, interviews, or funding are promised as prizes.

### Cited Findings
- Finals prizes: first place $100,000; second $50,000; third $25,000 (§5.2) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- "The top 5 teams at each regional receive $5,000 per team and advance to the Finals." plus "We'll also select up to five additional wildcard teams across the regional games based on their scores." (§5.1) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Prizes are per team, split equally between teammates; winners bear all taxes and must return documents within 14 days; payment within 30 days after verification (§5.3–5.4) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Sequoia partner @abhishekm1636 described it as "Over $250K in prizes." — [Digg](https://digg.com/ai/smdduv3c)
- Travel to the Finals is covered for the top teams from each regional — [Official site](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/); Sequoia's announcement says top five teams from each regional fly to the finals — [Digg](https://digg.com/ai/smdduv3c). The formal rules contain no travel commitment and say participants are responsible for their own expenses (§9.2, §9.4) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Format: 60-minute build window to write a Python bot implementing a provided Agent interface; starter kit includes template, local simulator, ICM calculator, payout structure, blind schedule (§3.3) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- The poker variant is confidential until the build phase begins; Finals use a different variant (§3.2, §5.2) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- AI coding tools: "Teams may use Astra and Codex to write, debug, and refine their bots during the build phase." (§3.4); AI may not make live gameplay decisions (§7.1); using OpenAI tools is optional (§7.3) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Bots run in isolated sandboxes with no network access; each decision must return within one millisecond; error/timeout = auto fold (or check if available); max file size 100 MB (§3.6–3.7) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- One submission per five minutes; latest accepted submission is the active bot; active bots are frozen at the start of each scored tournament (§3.5, §4.2) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Six scored tournaments T1–T6; T1 starts when the build phase ends, each next one 20 minutes later; each is 10,000 randomly seeded eight-bot sit-and-gos; 2,000 starting chips, blinds from 10/20 rising every 25 hands; table ends when one bot has all chips or after 200 hands (§4.1–4.4) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Scoring: points by finishing position (distribution announced at build start); at the 200-hand cap, remaining points are split by ICM; team score per tournament = average points per table; final score = T1×1 + T2×2 + T3×3 + T4×4 + T5×5 + T6×6 (§4.5, §4.7) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Ties resolved by an objective, skill-based tie-breaker challenge; rankings depend solely on bot performance (§4.8–4.9) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Bot state resets at each table, opponents are anonymized, bots play only bots; empty seats are filled with copies of submitted bots that earn no points (§3.8–3.9, §4.6) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Disqualification for collusion, copying code, platform interference, endpoint misuse, cheating, malicious code, etc.; disputes must be raised before the next scored tournament (T6: within 5 minutes of results); variant may not be publicly shared before the event ends (§6.1–6.4) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Participants keep ownership of their bot code; they grant a non-exclusive license to run/evaluate it and to use hand logs and anonymized results; no commercialization license (§8.1) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Chips and points have no cash value and no real money is bet; entry is free, no purchase necessary — [Zeniteq](https://www.zeniteq.com/sequoia-and-openai-open-a-100k-pokerbot-tournament-6aycef); [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)

### Inferences
- Total cash is consistent with the "over $250K" claim: 15 regional top-5 teams × $5,000 = $75,000, plus $175,000 in Finals prizes = $250,000 (own arithmetic; wildcard teams' $5,000 entitlement is not stated).
- Finals field is up to 20 teams (15 regional qualifiers + up to 5 wildcards) (own arithmetic, not stated in rules).
- A two-person winning team would receive $50,000 each before tax; a solo winner keeps the full $100,000.
- Because later tournaments are weighted up to 6x, iterating and resubmitting during play (every 5 minutes) matters more than the initial 60-minute build.
- The official landing page demo shows no-limit hold'em (8 bots, blinds 50/100; heads-up 10,000 hands), but the rules say the actual variant is secret, so the demo should not be assumed to be the tournament game.

### Gaps
- "Astra" in §3.4 could not be identified from the sources read (possibly an OpenAI coding tool/model; unverified). Whether other assistants (e.g., non-OpenAI tools) are allowed is not stated in what was retrieved.
- Exact travel terms for finalists (flights, lodging, caps) are not published; the landing page promise and the rules' silence differ.
- No job/interview/funding prize is stated anywhere; none should be assumed.
- Points distribution per finishing position and blind schedule details are only revealed at the event.

## 7. How to register; application/selection process

### Takeaway
Registration was via an application on pokerbot.sequoiacap.com (free, school email, teammate named at application), reviewed on a rolling basis with limited seats; an optional "Castle Challenge" mini-game gave top scorers priority. Applications are now closed for all three regionals.

### Cited Findings
- Register at pokerbot.sequoiacap.com; both teammates must register by the deadline; the teammate named in the application receives an email link to submit their own application (§2.1–2.2) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html); [Official site](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/)
- Each participant must accept the rules, acknowledge the privacy notice, and make a photo/video election (§2.1) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- Review is rolling with limited seats at each regional; acceptance notices by Nov 5 / Nov 8 / Nov 11 for Bay Area / Boston / Pittsburgh — [Official site](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/)
- Optional "Castle Challenge": about three minutes across two strategy games; applicants split 100 whole-number soldiers across five castles worth 1–5 points; top scorers receive priority registration — [Zeniteq](https://www.zeniteq.com/sequoia-and-openai-open-a-100k-pokerbot-tournament-6aycef)
- Entry is free; no purchase necessary (§1.5) — [Official rules](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/rules.html)
- "APPLICATIONS ARE NOW CLOSED." — [Official site](https://pokerbot.sequoiacap.com/)

### Inferences
- The Castle Challenge is a Colonel Blotto-style game used as a lightweight selection filter (characterization is my own).
- For anyone who missed the deadline, the only practical step is emailing pokerbot@sequoiacap.com (e.g., about waitlists or future editions); no waitlist is documented.

### Gaps
- Selection criteria beyond the Castle Challenge (school, resume, etc.), seat counts per regional, and acceptance rates are not published.
- The Castle Challenge detail comes from a single secondary source; it was not visible in the fetched summary of the (now closed) official page.
